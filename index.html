MEET YOUR COACH

I spent years not knowing how to train for my body, feeling lost in the gym and unsure how to fuel myself properly. I was putting in the effort, but I didn't have the knowledge.

Finding a coach completely changed my life. I learnt how to lift, fuel my body properly and, most importantly, understand why I was doing it.

A lot of the women I coach are in this exact same position.

My mission now is to help as many women as possible learn how to fuel themselves properly, lift confidently and understand their bodies.
