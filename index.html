MEET YOUR COACH

I spent years not knowing how to train for my body, feeling lost in the gym and unsure how to fuel myself properly. I was putting in the effort, but I didn't have the knowledge.

Finding a coach completely changed my life. I learnt how to lift, build muscle and, most importantly, fuel my body correctly.

A lot of the women I coach are in this exact same position.

My mission now is to help as many women as possible learn how to fuel themselves properly, lift confidently and better understand their bodies.
